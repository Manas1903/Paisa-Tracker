## Model notes

- Stored user-edited rewards replace the registry entry completely.
- Exclusions are checked before base or bonus rates. The estimate applies per-rule caps within the card's statement cycle when its statement day is set, otherwise calendar month.
- A capped rule stops earning beyond its cap unless the source explicitly says eligible spend resumes at the base rate.
- The transaction-entry card suggestion estimates rupee rewards for the entered amount after applying that card's existing billing-cycle reward caps, eligible-spend cap, and minimum transaction rule.
- The estimate does not import issuer MCC, MID or TID decisions. Keyword-based classifications are proxies and are called out above.
- No database migration is needed; all added rewards fields are optional in the existing cards JSON.
