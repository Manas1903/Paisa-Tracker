Changes made

Accounting correction

Card purchases show in the month they happened

The “Where it went” chart now includes credit-card purchases under their spending categories, along with investments. A later card-bill payment remains a transfer: it changes bank cash and card dues, but does not add a second expense.

Why: adding both the original swipes and the bill payment to a spending pie would double-count the same purchase. This follows the month-of-purchase accounting you described.

Statements

Safer classification and refunds

  • Ordinary utility BILLPAY rows stay expenses; recognized card payment wording and known-card IB BILLPAY rows become transfers.
  • Bank POS rows use debit-card mode, so they do not create credit-card dues.
  • Refund credits are saved as expenses with refund semantics, reducing spending, budgets, and the relevant card balance.
  • Rows with unknown debit/credit direction need review and cannot pass statement reconciliation as balanced.
  • Duplicate matching checks merchant and type. Stable import IDs plus upsert make a retry after a partial batch safe.
Cards and rewards

Caps and dues use the full card charge

  • Outstanding balances are calculated per card, so one card’s overpayment cannot hide another card’s due.
  • Refunds reduce the corresponding card exposure.
  • Split purchases use the full charged amount for reward limits and card exposure; spending still uses the user’s share.
  • Reward exclusions match complete words, and cap tracking keys each billing cycle by its actual start date.
Loans and dates

Less misleading balances and reminders

  • When principal, annual rate, and term are known, the net-worth loan value uses estimated remaining principal rather than all future EMI payments.
  • FD/RD estimates accrue by elapsed days; maturity dates clamp to valid month-end dates.
  • Card due dates and monthly calendar reminders handle short months.
Stocks and interface

Fixed refresh and interaction edge cases

  • Stock quote refresh dependencies are stable; saves are queued to prevent overlapping profile writes.
  • Future-dated stock purchases are excluded from current holdings. Same-day purchases do not show a meaningless annualized XIRR.
  • XIRR is labelled annualized.
  • Quick Add is scrollable on small screens, save errors release the busy state, dashboard transaction rows are buttons, and motion follows the system reduced-motion preference.
  • Common edit sheets and Quick Add have dialog semantics and keyboard focus containment.
Docs and database

Updated project guidance and prepared a migration

README, feature guide, and AGENTS now describe the corrected card-spend timing, refund behavior, import safety, annualized XIRR, and stock-data limits. A new Supabase migration adds refund storage, same-owner recurring links, and input-size constraints. The migration is not applied to the hosted database.

What I would not change blindly

Needs a workflow decision

FD/RD maturity and payout

I would not automatically remove a matured deposit or turn its full principal into income. It may have renewed, paid out, or remained in the account. The app still needs an explicit “record payout / renew” action that closes the investment and records the destination without counting principal as income. Until then, maturity values remain estimates and can be double-counted if the user logs proceeds but keeps the deposit asset.

Provider terms

Stock history is not a Google feed

The current chart uses TejHQ daily end-of-day data. Its terms describe free use as personal and require a paid plan for an app that shows data to other users. Exchange rights can apply separately under the NSE policy. I would confirm permission before exposing this to other users; I would not subscribe to a paid tier without your approval. The data is not guaranteed or intraday.

Needs an accounting model

Stock sales, taxes, and dividends

Buy lots work today, but sell lots need a cost-basis rule (FIFO or another method), partial-sale support, and dividend treatment. I would not encode tax or sale logic as negative share quantities without agreeing on that model first.

Estimate, not a guarantee

Bank-specific deposit terms

FD/RD estimates use the entered rate and compounding schedule. Issuers can use product-specific day counts, payout timing, tax withholding, and rounding. The receipt’s actual maturity amount should take priority; an estimate must not be presented as a bank quote.

Legacy data limitation

Loans without principal or rate

For older loan records without enough inputs, the app still has to use remaining scheduled EMIs as a proxy. That includes future interest and is not the same as the principal owed. New or edited loans with principal and rate get a better estimate; incomplete records should be labelled approximate.

Terms need current checking

Card reward presets

Caps and minimums can be modelled, but merchant keywords cannot know the issuer’s MCC/MID/TID classification. Brochures and issuer rules change. Treat the best-card result as an estimate, and re-check each card’s current official terms before relying on it.

Not changed in this pass

Hosted security settings and CSP

The review did not inspect the live Supabase authentication configuration. I did not change hosted auth settings. I also did not add a restrictive Content Security Policy without first inventorying the app’s required script, font, image, and API origins; an incomplete policy can break sign-in or data loading. The separate input-size and ownership checks are prepared in the migration, pending local/hosted database review.
