1 · credit cards

Credit cards, counted the right way

A swipe counts as spending the day you swipe. Paying the bill later only moves money from your bank to the card, so it is never counted twice.

Oct swipe SBI Pulse ₹30,000 → October spending · bank unchanged · card owes ₹30,000 Nov pay the bill ₹30,000 → not spending · bank −₹30,000 · card owes ₹0
  • Card dues per card: amount owed, % of limit (warns above 30% for your CIBIL score), days to due date, Pay bill button
  • Bank balance leaves card spends out until the bill is paid; net worth subtracts what you owe
  • Monthly spending counts the swipe month. The later bill payment appears as cash outflow in Where it went, not as spending again.
  • Where it went also shows investment outflows. Regular bills entered as expenses flow through Money Flow; card-bill transfers are excluded there to avoid double counting.

New · card rewards

Best-card hints apply reward caps

When you enter a card purchase, the hint compares your saved card rules with the merchant and amount. It accounts for cycle caps, minimum transactions, eligible-spend limits and exclusions. It can fall back to the base rate when the card terms allow it.

This is an estimate. Keyword matching cannot see the issuer's MCC, MID or TID decision. Check your card's current terms.

Find it Add expense → choose CardCards & settings → rewards
📈
New · stocks

Stock lots, P&L and XIRR

Record each NSE/BSE purchase with its date, symbol, shares and buy price. The portfolio calculates units, average cost, value, profit/loss and XIRR from the saved quote.

The chart uses daily end-of-day prices, refreshed on first view and about once per day while the page is open. You can also refresh manually. It is not an intraday feed or a broker connection.

Find it ＋ → Invest → StocksStocks card
🏦
New · fixed income

FD and RD maturity tracking

Enter the rate, term, compounding, payout plan, issuer and product. The app estimates maturity and shows the date. Add the receipt's amount and date when you have them; the receipt amount takes priority over the estimate. Choose whether to credit or renew at maturity.

Periodic interest payouts are shown separately from the deposit balance. Estimates are not bank quotes.

2 · import

Import bank & card statements

Upload a PDF, CSV or Excel statement. It is read on your phone only, and you check every row before anything is saved.

  • Opens password-protected PDFs
  • Bank statements prove themselves: opening + credits − debits = closing, and a misread row gets flagged
  • Skips entries already logged, unticks refunds, saves bill payments as transfers
  • Learns your category fixes per merchant

Higher or lower?

A 5-round quiz built from your own spending: pizza or fuel last month? cabs or food delivery this year?

Recurring & Mark paid

Loans/EMIs with rate, principal, tenure, estimated EMI and total repayment; SIPs, FDs/RDs, subscriptions, bills, rent and salary. Coming up lists the next 30 days with Mark paid / received / invested, overdue items in orange.

✎

Edit anything

Tap any transaction, EMI, SIP, subscription or goal to edit or delete it (two taps to delete). Editing a recurring item asks: from now on, or past + future.

📈

Mutual fund portfolio

Link a SIP to its fund by name. Units are worked out from each day's NAV; you see current value, gain, last-day change and an invested-vs-value trend. Prices refresh daily from AMFI data.

🏦

FD / RD portfolio

Track deposits with saved interest rate, term, compounding, payout plan and maturity instruction. The maturity estimate uses those terms; a receipt amount overrides the estimate when entered.

🔒

Logins & privacy

Email and password login. Every row is private to its owner, enforced by the database. Start fresh with your bank balance, or explore with demo data.

📱

iPhone app, free

Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Dark-friendly icon, full-screen, 16px inputs (no zoom on tap), no jumping cards, safe areas for the notch and home bar.
