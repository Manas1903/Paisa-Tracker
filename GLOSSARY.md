# Paisa Tracker

A personal-finance app for one owner and their friends: tracks money in, out, owed and invested, and turns good habits into games.

## Money

**Expense**:
Money spent on yourself. In a split, only your share is your expense, counted on the day of the purchase.
_Avoid_: Spend (when meaning a split's full charge)

**Card bill payment**:
Money moved from the bank to a credit card. It settles card dues and is never an expense.
_Avoid_: Card expense, bill (alone)

**Card due**:
What you still owe a card: every charge on it, including friends' shares, minus card bill payments.

## Friends

**Friend**:
A person you split money with or compare stats with. Either linked to an account, or name-only.

**Name-only friend**:
A friend with no account behind them, known only by the name you typed. Can be linked to an account later.
_Avoid_: Contact, guest

**Invite link**:
A single-use link, valid for 7 days; opening it makes the two people friends at once, after sign-up if the person has no account.
_Avoid_: Referral, invite email

**Split**:
A purchase whose cost is divided between you and one or more friends.
_Avoid_: Shared expense, group bill

**Participant**:
Anyone included in a split, including the payer. Every participant can edit or settle it.

**Payer**:
The participant who paid the merchant. Everyone else's share is owed to the payer.
_Avoid_: Lender, owner

**Share**:
One person's part of a split. A friend's share is money they owe the payer until settled.

**Settle**:
A friend paying back their share. The money counts as coming back to the payer's bank, not as income from work.
_Avoid_: Repay, clear

**Split update**:
A note shown to every other participant the next time they open the app, saying what changed in a split (added, edited, settled).
_Avoid_: Notification, alert

## Games

**Shared stats**:
The percentages and scores a user lets a friend see: savings rate, invest rate, spending by category as % of total, goal progress %, report card grade, fair score, level, streaks. Never rupee amounts. One switch per friend, off until turned on.
_Avoid_: Profile, public data

**Fair score**:
A monthly 0–100 score built only from ratios and habits, so earning more never ranks you higher by itself. Used for the friends leaderboard.
_Avoid_: Rank, wealth score
